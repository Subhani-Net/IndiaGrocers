# CATEGORY 3: EVERYDAY FOUNDATIONAL SPICES & POWDERS

> Procurement perspective: Spices are repeat-purchase essentials. TRS and East End
> dominate UK cash-and-carry (Dhamecha, Bestway) and are the default choice for
> price-sensitive buyers. MDH and Everest are trusted quality markers for Indians
> who grew up with those brands. Stock 400g and 1kg on high-velocity lines
> (Turmeric, Chilli, Coriander, Garam Masala). MDH Deggi Mirch is a specialist line
> that serious cooks specifically seek — always have it. Asafoetida (Hing) is small
> volume but near 100% repurchase rate; never be out of stock.
