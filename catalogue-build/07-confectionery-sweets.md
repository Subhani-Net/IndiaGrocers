# CATEGORY 7: CONFECTIONERY & SWEETS

> Procurement perspective: Indian sweets are a gifting and celebration category —
> Diwali, Eid, Raksha Bandhan, and family gatherings drive massive volume spikes.
> Stock up 3–4 weeks before Diwali. Ambient tub sweets (Royal/Quality) are everyday
> impulse buys after dinner. Tinned Gulab Jamun (Haldiram's, MTR) is a regular
> household staple — it's eaten at least weekly in most homes. Soan Papdi is the
> single highest-selling Diwali gifting product in the UK Indian community.
> Indian chocolates (Cadbury Indian variants) are nostalgic import lines that
> carry significant premium over UK variants — sell them on the emotional hook.
> Hajmola is a near-pharmacy essential; stock it in sweets AND in health/snacks.
