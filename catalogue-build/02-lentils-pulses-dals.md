# CATEGORY 2: LENTILS, PULSES & DALS

> Procurement perspective: Dals are a daily staple — every household buys at least
> 2–3 varieties weekly. Stock both oily and dry toor dal (strong regional preference:
> Gujarati/Punjabi households prefer dry, South Indian households prefer oily).
> Urad dal black whole (for dal makhani) is high-turnover. Always carry 5kg packs
> of Toor, Chana, and Moong as a minimum. Kabuli chana canned is an impulse line
> for weeknight cooks. Kala Chana spikes at Karva Chauth and Navratri.
