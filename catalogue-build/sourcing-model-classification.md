# Sourcing Model Classification — Floating C&C vs Managed Inventory

**Last updated:** May 2026

---

## The Four Sourcing Tiers

Every SKU in the 242-item MVC falls into one of four tiers:

| Tier | Label | What It Means | Action |
|------|-------|--------------|--------|
| **A** | Pure Floating | Always on shelf at TRS / Natco / Dhamecha / Bestway. Buy per order, same day. | List freely. No buffer stock needed. |
| **B** | Floating — Call Ahead | Usually in C&C but can have gaps. 1–2 day lead time. | List freely but set safety stock of 2–4 units at home. |
| **C** | Managed Inventory — Specialist Import | Comes from specialist South Indian / Pakistani importer. MOQ applies. Buy fortnightly. | Hold 2–3 weeks' cover. Order on a schedule. |
| **D** | Managed Inventory — Frozen / Cold Chain | Requires freezer storage + cold delivery infrastructure before listing. | Do NOT list until cold chain is set up. Phase 2 only. |

## TIER D — Managed Inventory / Cold Chain (Do Not List Until Infrastructure Ready)

**20 SKUs. Requires freezer storage at your dispatch point AND insulated
last-mile delivery (insulated boxes + ice packs, or refrigerated van).
These are high-stickiness lines — especially Shana frozen — but they
cannot be listed until the cold chain is solved. This is Phase 2.**

> **What you need before listing Tier D:**
> 1. **Chest freezer** — ~£200–350 (second-hand commercial) or £120–200 (new domestic 200L)
> 2. **Insulated delivery boxes** — Woolcool or EPS foam boxes with gel ice packs (~£2–4/box)
> 3. **Same-day or next-day frozen delivery window** — don't ship frozen on a Friday
> 4. **Shana distributor account** — contact Shana Foods UK direct (Bradford).
>    They have a UK distributor network; don't buy from Bestway retail — get trade pricing.
>
> **Estimated frozen infrastructure cost:** £300–600 one-off
> **Additional delivery cost per frozen order:** £1.50–3.00 (insulated packing)
>
> **Target: list Tier D by Month 2–3** once you've proven ambient order volume.
