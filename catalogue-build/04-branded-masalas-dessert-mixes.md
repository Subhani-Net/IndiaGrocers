# CATEGORY 4: BRANDED RECIPE MASALAS & DESSERT MIXES

> Procurement perspective: Masalas are an emotional purchase — brand loyalty is
> fierce and regional. Shan dominates Pakistani households in Tooting and Croydon
> (Biryani, Nihari, Karahi are the top 3). MDH is trusted by North Indian
> Punjabi/Delhi households — Kitchen King and Chana Masala are perennial top
> sellers. Everest is the Maharashtrian/Gujarati marker — Pav Bhaji and Sambhar
> Masala are essential. Keep full Shan range in depth. Laziza dessert mixes
> peak sharply at Eid, Diwali, and Ramadan — overprepare for these windows.
