# CATEGORY 1: RICE & ATTA (FLOURS)

> Procurement perspective: South London communities (Croydon, Sutton, Tooting) are
> high-volume purchasers of 10kg and 20kg atta sacks and 10–20kg basmati. Stock
> deep on Tilda, Elephant, Aashirvaad, Lal Qilla. Idli rice and parboiled varieties
> are essential for South Indian households concentrated in Tooting Broadway.
