# CATEGORY 8: BEVERAGES, TEAS & COFFEES

> Procurement perspective: Tea is the single most purchased grocery item in South
> Asian UK households — families drink 5–8 cups daily and buy tea in 500g–1kg
> quantities monthly. Never be out of Brooke Bond Red Label, Wagh Bakri, or Tata
> Gold. Loose leaf massively outsells teabags in this demographic — price the
> 1kg format aggressively as a known-value item. South Indian filter coffee
> (Udhayam, Continental, Leo) is essential for Tamil households in Tooting —
> a key differentiation point against mainstream supermarkets who carry none.
> Mango juice / Maaza and Rooh Afza are near-universal purchases; spike Rooh Afza
> orders heavily for Ramadan (400% normal demand). Bovonto is cult-level for
> Tamil customers — stock it year-round.
