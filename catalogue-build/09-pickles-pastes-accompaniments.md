# CATEGORY 9: PICKLES, COOKING PASTES & ACCOMPANIMENTS

> Procurement perspective: Pickles are a permanent pantry item — every household
> has 2–3 jars open at any time. Priya is the dominant South Indian brand for
> the Andhra/Telangana community; Aachi is key for Tamil households. Patak's is
> the household crossover brand that even non-South Asian shoppers recognise —
> stock deep in Mango Chutney and Lime Pickle. Ginger-garlic paste (1kg) is a
> weekly purchase at near-restaurant scale — it's the single product that drives
> the biggest basket uplift after atta. Ching's Secret is cult-level for the
> Indo-Chinese food trend that runs across all age demographics. Maggi Hot &
> Sweet Sauce is the original Indian-made tomato-chilli sauce that competes with
> Heinz ketchup in desi households — always stock the 1kg size.
