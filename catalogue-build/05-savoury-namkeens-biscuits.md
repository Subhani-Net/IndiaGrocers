# CATEGORY 5: SAVOURY NAMKEENS & PREMIUM BISCUITS

> Procurement perspective: Namkeens are impulse and gifting lines. 200g is the
> everyday eat; 400g/1kg is the bulk/gifting format. Haldiram's is the premium
> market leader — position it prominently. Bikaji is the challenger brand with
> strong price-value perception. Papads (Lijjat) are a near-100% pantry staple
> for every household. Indian biscuits (Parle-G, Britannia Good Day, Karachi Bakery)
> are near-weekly repeat purchases — treat them like essentials, not confectionery.
> Karachi Bakery Fruit Biscuits spike massively at Diwali as gifting boxes.
