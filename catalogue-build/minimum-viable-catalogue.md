# Minimum Viable Catalogue (MVC) — Phase 1

**Model:** Virtual storefront, C&C-fulfilled (TRS, Natco, Haldiram's UK, Dhamecha/Bestway)
**Target:** South London — Croydon, Sutton, Tooting
**Goal:** ~220 SKUs that achieve basket completeness, repeat ordering, and differentiation
**Last updated:** May 2026

---

## The Three Rules for Every SKU on This List

1. **Reliably available** at TRS or Natco C&C (no exotic imports that disappear)
2. **Earns its place** — it is either an anchor, a weekly trigger, or a differentiator
3. **Someone in South London buys it every week** — no enthusiast-only lines at this stage

---

## Why 220 SKUs and Not More

An average South Asian household basket has 15–25 items per order. You need to be able
to fill 80%+ of it. Below 150 SKUs you can't — customers will fill gaps elsewhere and
you lose them. Above 350 SKUs in phase 1, your C&C run time per order becomes
unmanageable and you start listing items that are inconsistently stocked.

220 SKUs gives basket completeness without C&C chaos.

## What to Add in Phase 2 (After 90 Days Sales Data)

Review which categories are generating repeat orders and expand those first:

- If Shan masalas sell fast → expand to full 30-SKU Shan range
- If South Indian teas/coffee sell fast → add Girnar herbal, Nescafe Gold, Bournvita
- If pickle orders are concentrated on Priya → add Aachi full range + National full range
- If frozen drives reorders → add Kawan, more Shana stuffed parathas
- If sweets sell → add ambient tub sweets (Royal Sweets) and expand Haldiram's tins

**Never add a SKU that you cannot reliably source at your C&C within 24 hours of an order.**
