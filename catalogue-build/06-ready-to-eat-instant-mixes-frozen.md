# CATEGORY 6: READY-TO-EAT, INSTANT MIXES & FROZEN BREADS

> Procurement perspective: Ready meals are the fastest-growing segment in UK South
> Asian grocery — driven by time-poor second and third generation shoppers. Ashoka
> is the premium ambient leader; position it as a quality alternative to home
> cooking. MTR breakfast mixes are non-negotiable for South Indian households —
> Tooting is heavy Tamil/Telugu/Kannada community; Idli and Dosa mixes are weekly
> repurchases. Shana frozen parathas and rotis are the highest-volume frozen lines
> in any South Asian store — stock at least 6 variants deep and never let them
> go out of stock. Frozen samosas (Shana, East End) drive basket uplift.
